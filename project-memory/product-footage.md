2026-10-24, after «iPhone Duo» v2 (approved, uploading). User: if a video is about iPhones or similar things, find videos about it online. For example, the official trailer Apple usually releases. Insert real product footage, not stock. Save it in the textbook.

**Why:** a graphics-only mockup looks weaker than the real product shots.

**How to apply:**
- At the material stage, search for the official trailer/promo/keynote of the named product (fall back to reviews).
- If the cloud can't download it, use yt-dlp on the PC → `_inbox\<date>\footage\`.
- Insert 0.6–2 s b-roll clips under the matching words. Mute the footage audio (no trailer music). Crop to 9:16, no watermarks.
- For client ads, ask about rights.
- Vault rule: vault/01-rules/product-footage.md (SYSTEM п.15).
