2026-10-20 user asked for full pro training on editing real vlog footage ("текст следить за головой", full SFX, no AI look). Guide: vault/03-techniques/talking-head-pro-guide.md (also on PC vault).

Key points: build a "person map" first (MediaPipe face/pose/hands → face_track.json, pose_track.json, gestures.csv; RVM/MatAnyone matte; AE fallback Face Tracking Detail Analysis, Mocha AE). Text levels У1-У5: captions that dodge the face, head-locked label (parent to track null, smoothed, 30-50% of head roll), text behind person only on peaks, planar-tracked text on wall, gesture-driven text. SFX per cut/push-in/gesture shaped to the motion, panned to its side, 10-16 dB under voice. Cut on blink/action. Supersedes live-editing-v3's "no push-in" line (see camera-allowed). See [[edit-system-v3]], [[ai-look-audit]].
